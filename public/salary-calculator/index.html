📑 Model Blueprint: Profit sharing post GST and expenses Corporate Governance & Financial Policy

The Profit sharing post GST and expenses model was designed specifically for Solve Your Trip to eliminate rigid, arbitrary commission slabs and replace them with complete fiscal control at the organizational level.

1. 18% Inclusive GST Extraction

Since client payments and invoice margins are inclusive of 18% GST, statutory tax liability is calculated as Base = Gross / 1.18 and GST = Gross - Base. The company never pays incentives on government tax money.

2. Sunk Overhead Recovery

Marketing (₹12K), Misc (₹4K), and Ops costs (₹10K) incurred behind the sales representative are covered first. The company guarantees its operating costs are 100% recovered before calculating surplus.

3. Base Salary Protection

The employee's guaranteed fixed base salary is paid from net operating margin. Only remaining revenue above base salary constitutes Clocked Net Company Profit.

4. Discretionary Profit Disbursal %

Management chooses what percentage (e.g. 15%, 20%, 25%, or custom slabs) of this clocked surplus to disburse. Solve Your Trip retains 75% to 85% cash in bank, aligning rep rewards with pure company profit.
