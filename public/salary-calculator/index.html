📑 Model Policy Blueprint: 5X Justification & Extra Profit Slabs Official Company Compensation Framework

This official model codifies the finalized agreement between organization management and sales employees at Solve Your Trip.

1. The 5X Salary Justification Rule

An employee's monthly base salary is justified when they generate at least 5 times their base salary in gross profit (inclusive of 18% GST). E.g. A ₹20,000 salary requires ₹1,00,000 profit; ₹25,000 requires ₹1,25,000; ₹30,000 requires ₹1,50,000.

2. Statutory GST & Sunk Overhead Recovery

Gross profit includes 18% GST (Tax = Gross × 0.18 / 1.18). Fixed overheads incurred behind the sales representative (Marketing ₹12,000, Misc ₹4,000, Ops ₹10,000 = ₹26,000 total) are fully recovered by the company.

3. Incentives Exclusively on Extra Profit

No incentives are disbursed on the base 5X justification volume. Incentives are awarded strictly on the Extra Profit clocked above the 5X threshold (e.g. at ₹1,50,000 gross with ₹20K base, incentive applies only to the extra ₹50,000).

4. Bounded Slabs (20% Min to 50% Max)

Incentive percentages start at a minimum of 20% and are strictly capped at 50%. Rates exceeding 50% are prohibited to ensure the company preserves a healthy profit margin in all scenarios.
