🌟 Why the 30K Senior Rep Earns Higher Incentive:

Senior consultants are hired for their deep travel knowledge, high itinerary conversions, and ability to sell high-margin Maldives & Mauritius luxury packages. Giving them a higher disbursal percentage (25%-30% vs 15%) ensures they feel rewarded for their senior status and stay fiercely loyal to Solve Your Trip!

🛡️ Guaranteed Company Cash Retention:

Because all reductions (18% GST, ₹26K fixed overheads, and base salary) are deducted before the incentive pool is computed, the company retains 70% to 85% of net operating profit directly into company accounts. The company is completely immune to losses!
