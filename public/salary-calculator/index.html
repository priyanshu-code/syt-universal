💡 Why the 7x Target is Crucial:

In travel sales, high customer acquisition costs (Google/Meta Ads, quoting infrastructure, support) mean a sales rep must generate a solid multiplier to absorb cancellation risks and sustain company growth. At 7x, the company yields ~67% net margin on the sales unit.

🛡️ The Rule 4 Safety Net:

If a sales employee generates ₹40,000 profit (which is below the ₹56,098 threshold for 20K), post-GST net profit is only ₹32,800. After ₹26,000 overheads, only ₹6,800 remains. Paying full ₹20,000 salary would cause a -₹13,200 loss to Solve Your Trip! The threshold safeguards company solvency.
